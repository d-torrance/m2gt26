# Shaping the Future of Macaulay2
## Wednesday, May 13 - Friday, May 15, 2026
### Invitation Only

An invitation-only planning workshop will gather a small group of leaders and influential contributors to discuss long-term directions for Macaulay2. Participants will explore new mathematical and computational directions, opportunities to modernize the system’s architecture, connections with other research tools, and strategies for sustaining a vibrant developer and user community.

# Building the Future of Macaulay2
## Sunday, May 17 - Thursday, May 21, 2026
### Open to the Community

Immediately following the planning workshop, a traditional, open Macaulay2 workshop will take place. Students, researchers, and developers are invited to collaborate on a variety of Macaulay2 projects, with an emphasis on internal and infrastructure-related topics. Participants will have opportunities to learn, contribute code, explore mathematical applications, and work closely with experienced developers.

Together, these events aim to strengthen the Macaulay2 ecosystem, spark new ideas, and support the next generation of contributors.
